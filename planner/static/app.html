Try these mid-flight to see the safety states.
Start

Takes off on its own if on the ground, hands control to the app, then follows every vertex of the planned path using GPS and the barometer. It never cuts corners.

Safety

Any RC stick input kills the mission at once and locks the app out until Re-arm. It holds and hovers on stale GPS, fewer than 10 satellites, low battery, or drifting off the path or off the planned height.

Camera

Faces the direction of travel, or looks at the nearest look-at point (yaw and gimbal tilt). FPV style keeps moving through gentle corners with a fixed tilt.
